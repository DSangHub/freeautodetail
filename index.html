The 30-day loop

How it works

1

Seller books a no-cost detail

A local owner planning to sell or trade books a full detail at a participating shop. A credit card hold covers the detail price for 30 days.

2

The detail completes

The shop does its normal full detail. Founding Partner dealers get first notification the moment it's done — year, make, model, and area.

3

Dealer inspects locally

Interested dealers invite the seller in. You inspect a freshly detailed car at your own shop, on your own lift, on your own terms.

4

Deal closes, dealer pays

If the seller trades in or sells to a participating dealer within 30 days, that dealer pays the detail plus a $150 referral fee. The seller's hold is released.

If no deal happens in 30 days: the seller's card hold captures the detail price, the shop is still paid in full, and the seller keeps a professionally detailed car. No one goes unpaid.

The fine print, in plain English

  • Card hold. Sellers authorize a hold equal to the detail price for 30 days. Trade in or sell to a participating dealer within 30 days and the hold is released — the buying dealer pays the detail. Otherwise the hold captures and the seller pays for their detail.
  • Dealer fees. Participating dealers pay only when a deal closes: the actual detail price ($150–$300) plus a $150 FreeAutoDetail referral fee.
  • Shop requirements. Participating detail shops must maintain current general liability insurance and a valid business license at all times.
  • Liability. FreeAutoDetail.com is a referral and notification platform. It does not perform detailing services and is not responsible for damages incurred at participating detail shops. Claims for on-site damage are handled by the shop and its liability insurance.
  • No purchase obligation. Dealers are never obligated to buy, and sellers are never obligated to sell. The 30-day window simply decides who pays for the detail.
